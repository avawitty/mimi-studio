# Evidence for Mimi

Research board for Mimi Tailor proof-of-concepts. Not competitors — evidence.

## Interaction

- Taste selection interfaces with accept/reject/rename
- Editable pattern graphs with confidence visualizations
- Evidence-linked explanations on every inference
- Node editing (merge, split, weight)
- Museum-drawer signal inventory UI

## Visual Identity

- Editorial reports (fashion dossiers, magazine spreads)
- Museum labels and archive finding aids
- Artist archives and library catalogs
- Research notebooks (Field Notes)
- Black/cream premium aesthetic

## Animation

- Reflective eyes as poetic motif (not literal reading mechanic)
- Soft blinking, breathing idle motions
- Paper unfolding, ink appearing
- Constellation graphs growing
- Thread connections between evidence nodes
- Rive prototyping for lightweight motion

## Reports

- Scientific figures with claim-type badges
- Creative Dossier sections
- Archive finding aids
- Fashion dossiers
- Museum exhibition guides

## Education

- Explainability on every claim
- Annotation systems for user corrections
- Concept maps (Pattern Graph)
- Art history thematic comparisons (never identity mapping)
- Research workflows: observe → curate → transform

## Product Constitution

Mimi does not generate identity. Mimi reveals evidence.
The success metric is not imitation. The success metric is confidence.
